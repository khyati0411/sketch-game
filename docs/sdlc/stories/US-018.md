**As a** player, **I want** to rejoin the game if my Wi-Fi drops for a moment, **so that** I do not lose my place or my points.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Back within 60 s**
- **Given** I am in a game and my connection drops
- **When** I reconnect from the same browser within 60 seconds
- **Then** I am back in the game with my seat, score and the current canvas

**AC2: Seat released**
- **Given** my connection dropped
- **When** I have not come back after 60 seconds
- **Then** my seat is released and the others see that I left

**AC3: Others informed**
- **Given** a player's connection drops
- **When** they are away
- **Then** other players see them marked as reconnecting

## Notes
Guests are recognised by a random session token stored in the browser (design doc §5).
