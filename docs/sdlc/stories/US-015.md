**As a** guesser, **I want** to earn more points for guessing quickly, **so that** there is a reason to be fast.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Speed matters**
- **Given** two guessers guess the same word
- **When** one guesses after 10 seconds and the other after 60
- **Then** the faster guesser earns more points

**AC2: Drawer points**
- **Given** I am the drawer
- **When** some guessers get my word
- **Then** I earn points for each player who guessed it

**AC3: No guess, no points**
- **Given** the turn ends
- **When** I did not guess the word
- **Then** I earn no points for that turn

## Notes
The exact formula is fixed in a design-stage ADR and repeated in the test cases.
