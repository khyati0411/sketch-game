**As a** player, **I want** to see everyone who has joined the lobby, with their avatar color, as it changes, **so that** I know when my friends have arrived.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Live list**
- **Given** I am in a lobby
- **When** another player joins or leaves
- **Then** the player list updates within a second without reloading

**AC2: Host marked**
- **Given** I am in a lobby
- **When** I look at the player list
- **Then** the host is marked as host and each player shows their nickname and avatar color
