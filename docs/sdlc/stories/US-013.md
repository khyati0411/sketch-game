**As a** player, **I want** each turn to end clearly and show the word, **so that** everyone knows what was drawn before the next turn.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Everyone guessed**
- **Given** all guessers have guessed the word
- **When** the last correct guess arrives
- **Then** the turn ends straight away

**AC2: Time is up**
- **Given** not everyone has guessed
- **When** the countdown reaches 0
- **Then** the turn ends

**AC3: Reveal**
- **Given** a turn has ended
- **When** the turn summary shows
- **Then** every player sees the word and the points each player earned this turn for a few seconds
