**As a** drawer, **I want** to erase mistakes or start the drawing over, **so that** I can fix my drawing.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Eraser**
- **Given** it is my turn to draw
- **When** I pick the eraser and drag over part of the drawing
- **Then** that part is erased for everyone

**AC2: Clear**
- **Given** it is my turn to draw
- **When** I choose "Clear" and confirm
- **Then** the canvas is empty for everyone
