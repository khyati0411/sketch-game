**As a** guesser, **I want** to see strokes with no noticeable lag even in a full room, **so that** guessing feels fair and fun.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Latency target**
- **Given** a room has 8 players on the host PC's network
- **When** the drawer draws continuously for 30 seconds
- **Then** strokes reach guessers in under 150 ms at the 95th percentile

## Notes
Non-functional story for initiative goal G2. Measured by an automated multi-browser test.
