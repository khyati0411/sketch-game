**As a** player, **I want** to see everyone's score during the game, **so that** I know who is winning.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Scoreboard**
- **Given** a game is running
- **When** I look at the side panel
- **Then** I see every player with their score, highest first, and who is drawing

**AC2: Updates**
- **Given** a turn ends
- **When** points are awarded
- **Then** the scoreboard updates for every player

**AC3: Guessed marker**
- **Given** a turn is running
- **When** a player guesses the word
- **Then** they are marked as having guessed on everyone's scoreboard
