**As a** guesser, **I want** to type guesses in the chat and know when I am right, **so that** I can score points.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Wrong guess**
- **Given** it is a drawing turn and I am guessing
- **When** I send a wrong guess
- **Then** everyone sees my message in the chat

**AC2: Correct guess**
- **Given** the word is "cat"
- **When** I send "Cat " (any case, extra spaces)
- **Then** I am told I guessed it, everyone sees "<name> guessed the word", and my guess text is not shown to others

**AC3: After guessing**
- **Given** I have already guessed the word
- **When** I keep chatting
- **Then** my messages are only shown to the drawer and other players who already guessed

**AC4: Drawer cannot guess**
- **Given** I am the drawer
- **When** I type in the chat
- **Then** my message is not treated as a guess and the word is never revealed by it
