**As a** player, **I want** to join my friend's room by opening the link and picking a nickname, **so that** I can play without creating an account.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Join with a nickname**
- **Given** I opened a valid invite link
- **When** I enter a nickname of 1–20 characters and choose "Join"
- **Then** I enter the room's lobby and every player there sees me arrive

**AC2: Nickname required**
- **Given** I opened a valid invite link
- **When** I try to join with an empty nickname
- **Then** I cannot join and I am told a nickname is required

**AC3: Duplicate nickname**
- **Given** a player called "Sam" is already in the room
- **When** I join as "Sam"
- **Then** I am asked to choose a different nickname

**AC4: Room full**
- **Given** the room already has 8 players
- **When** I try to join
- **Then** I am told the room is full and I do not enter it

**AC5: Unknown room**
- **Given** the room code in the link does not exist
- **When** I open the link
- **Then** I am told the room was not found and can create my own room instead
