**As a** guesser, **I want** to see how many letters the word has and how much time is left, **so that** I can plan my guesses.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Blanks**
- **Given** a turn has started with the word "cat"
- **When** I look above the canvas
- **Then** I see "_ _ _" and the drawer sees the word itself

**AC2: Spaces shown**
- **Given** the word is "ice cream"
- **When** I look at the blanks
- **Then** the space between the two words is shown

**AC3: Countdown**
- **Given** a turn has started
- **When** time passes
- **Then** every player sees the same countdown from 80 seconds
