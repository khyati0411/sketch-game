**As a** player, **I want** to join a game that has already started, **so that** I do not have to wait for the next game.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Join mid-game**
- **Given** a game is in progress with fewer than 8 players
- **When** I open the invite link and join
- **Then** I see the current canvas, blanks, timer and scoreboard

**AC2: Guess at once**
- **Given** I joined during a turn
- **When** I guess the word correctly
- **Then** I score normally

**AC3: Draw from next round**
- **Given** I joined during round 1
- **When** round 2 starts
- **Then** I am included in the drawing order
