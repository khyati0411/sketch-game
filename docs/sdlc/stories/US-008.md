**As a** drawer, **I want** to fill a closed area with the current color, **so that** I can color in big shapes quickly.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Fill**
- **Given** it is my turn and the canvas has a closed shape
- **When** I pick the fill bucket and click inside the shape
- **Then** the inside of the shape fills with the current color for everyone

**AC2: Fill the background**
- **Given** it is my turn to draw
- **When** I use the fill bucket on the empty background
- **Then** the whole background takes the current color
