**As a** player, **I want** to see the final ranking when the game ends, **so that** we know who won.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Final ranking**
- **Given** the last turn of the last round has ended
- **When** the game finishes
- **Then** every player sees the final scores ranked, with the winner highlighted

**AC2: Ties**
- **Given** two players have the same top score
- **When** the final scoreboard shows
- **Then** both are shown as winners

**AC3: Play again**
- **Given** the final scoreboard is showing
- **When** the host chooses "Back to lobby"
- **Then** everyone returns to the lobby with scores reset
