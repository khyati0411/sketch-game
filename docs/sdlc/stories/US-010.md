**As a** drawer, **I want** to choose the word I will draw from three options, **so that** I can pick one I know how to draw.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Three choices**
- **Given** my turn to draw begins
- **When** the word choice appears
- **Then** I see three different words from the word list and others see "<name> is choosing a word"

**AC2: Pick a word**
- **Given** I see three words
- **When** I choose one
- **Then** my drawing turn starts with that word and the timer starts

**AC3: Auto-pick**
- **Given** I see three words
- **When** I do not choose within 15 seconds
- **Then** one of the three is picked for me and my turn starts

## Notes
Uses a built-in English word list for families. Words are not repeated within one game.
