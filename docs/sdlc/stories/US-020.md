**As a** player, **I want** the game to keep going sensibly when someone leaves, **so that** one person leaving does not ruin it for everyone.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Drawer leaves**
- **Given** a turn is in progress
- **When** the drawer leaves
- **Then** the turn ends at once, the word is revealed and nobody scores for it

**AC2: Host leaves**
- **Given** I am in a room
- **When** the host leaves
- **Then** the player who has been in the room longest becomes host and everyone sees it

**AC3: Too few players**
- **Given** a game is running with 2 players
- **When** one of them leaves
- **Then** the game ends and the remaining player sees the final scoreboard
