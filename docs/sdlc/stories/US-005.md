**As a** drawer, **I want** to draw freehand on the canvas while everyone else watches it appear, **so that** they can guess what I am drawing.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Freehand drawing**
- **Given** it is my turn to draw
- **When** I drag with the mouse or my finger on the canvas
- **Then** a smooth line follows my pointer

**AC2: Live for guessers**
- **Given** I am drawing
- **When** I draw a stroke
- **Then** every guesser sees the same stroke appear as I draw it, not only when I lift the pen

**AC3: Only the drawer draws**
- **Given** it is not my turn to draw
- **When** I drag on the canvas
- **Then** nothing is drawn

**AC4: Same picture everywhere**
- **Given** a turn has ended
- **When** players compare their canvases
- **Then** every canvas shows the same drawing

## Notes
Works with mouse and touch. The sync model is decided in the R1 design stage (ADR).
