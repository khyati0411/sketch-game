**As a** drawer, **I want** to pick a color and a brush size, **so that** my drawing is easier to recognise.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Colors**
- **Given** it is my turn to draw
- **When** I pick a color from the palette
- **Then** my next strokes use that color, for me and for guessers

**AC2: Brush sizes**
- **Given** it is my turn to draw
- **When** I pick one of the brush sizes
- **Then** my next strokes use that thickness, for me and for guessers

## Notes
Palette of about 12–20 colors and 3–5 sizes; exact values are a design-stage decision.
