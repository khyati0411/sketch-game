**As a** drawer, **I want** to undo my last stroke, fill or clear, **so that** one slip does not ruin my drawing.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1: Undo**
- **Given** I have drawn several strokes this turn
- **When** I choose "Undo"
- **Then** the last stroke disappears for everyone

**AC2: Repeated undo**
- **Given** I have drawn three strokes this turn
- **When** I choose "Undo" three times
- **Then** the canvas is back to how it was at the start of my turn

**AC3: Nothing to undo**
- **Given** I have not drawn anything this turn
- **When** I look at "Undo"
- **Then** it is disabled
