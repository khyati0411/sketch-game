## Summary
The game copes with real home Wi-Fi: dropped connections, people leaving or arriving mid-game, and up to eight players drawing and guessing without lag.

## Stories
- US-018: Reconnecting player keeps their seat and score
- US-019: Late joiners can play straight away
- US-020: Game handles the drawer or host leaving
- US-021: Drawing stays real-time with eight players
- US-022: Server handles twenty full rooms at once

The live list with status is on the dashboard and in the backlog (`npm run sdlc:backlog`).

## Done when
Reconnects, late joins and departures follow the room rules in the design doc, and the latency and load targets (G2, G3) are met.
