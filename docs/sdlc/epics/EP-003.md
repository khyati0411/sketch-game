## Summary
Players take turns drawing a word they picked from three choices while everyone else guesses it in chat against the clock.

## Stories
- US-010: Drawer chooses one of three words
- US-011: Guessers see the word as blanks and a countdown
- US-012: Guessers guess in chat and correct guesses are recognized
- US-013: Turn ends and the word is revealed
- US-014: Turns rotate through every player for three rounds

The live list with status is on the dashboard and in the backlog (`npm run sdlc:backlog`).

## Done when
A full round runs on its own: each player draws once, words are picked, guesses are judged, and turns end and rotate without anyone stepping in.
