## Objective
Messages after a correct guess, and the drawer's messages, never reach players still guessing (US-012 AC3, AC4).

## Preconditions
A drawing turn with word "cat"; guesser 1 has guessed it, guesser 2 has not.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guesser 1 sends "so easy" | The drawer sees it; guesser 2 does not |
| 2 | Drawer sends "cat" | It is not counted as a guess, and guesser 2 does not see the word |
