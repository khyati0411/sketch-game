## Objective
A drawer leaving ends the turn with no points, including guesses already made (US-020 AC1, ADR-005).

## Preconditions
A drawing turn; one guesser has already guessed.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The drawer closes their tab | The turn ends at once; the word is revealed; the guesser's points for the turn are removed |
