## Objective
Erasing and clearing change every canvas (US-007 AC1, AC2).

## Preconditions
A drawing turn; the drawer has drawn a black stroke.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drawer picks the eraser and drags over half the stroke | That half is white on both canvases |
| 2 | Drawer chooses "Clear" and confirms | Both canvases are blank |
