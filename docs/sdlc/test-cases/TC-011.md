## Objective
The action log gives every player the same picture (US-005 AC4, ADR-003).

## Preconditions
A drawing turn with drawer and two guessers.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drawer draws several strokes in two colors, fills one area and undoes one stroke | Strokes appear for everyone |
| 2 | Let the turn end; capture each canvas | All canvases match at reduced resolution within the ADR-003 pixel-difference threshold |
