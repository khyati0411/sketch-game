## Objective
Flood fill works inside shapes and on the empty background (US-008 AC1, AC2).

## Preconditions
A drawing turn; the drawer has drawn a closed square.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drawer picks yellow and the fill tool, clicks inside the square | The inside is yellow on both canvases; the outside stays white |
| 2 | Drawer picks blue and clicks outside the square | The background is blue on both canvases; the square stays yellow |
