## Objective
Joining mid-game works as in ADR-004 (US-019 AC1–AC3).

## Preconditions
Test timers; a game in round 1 with two players and strokes on the canvas.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A third player joins from the invite link | They see the current canvas, the blanks, the timer and the scoreboard |
| 2 | They guess the word | They score like any guesser |
| 3 | Round 2 starts | They are in the drawing order this round |
