## Objective
Guess matching ignores case and spaces, and the word never leaks (US-012 AC1, AC2).

## Preconditions
A drawing turn with word "cat", drawer and two guessers.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guesser 1 sends "dog" | All players see "Guesser 1: dog" |
| 2 | Guesser 1 sends "Cat " | Guesser 1 sees "You guessed it!"; everyone sees "Guesser 1 guessed the word!" |
| 3 | Check guesser 2's chat | The text "Cat" does not appear |
