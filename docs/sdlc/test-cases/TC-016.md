## Objective
Undo removes the latest action everywhere (US-009 AC1–AC3).

## Preconditions
A new drawing turn.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Look at "Undo" | It is disabled |
| 2 | Draw three strokes, then choose "Undo" once | The third stroke disappears on both canvases |
| 3 | Choose "Undo" twice more | Both canvases are blank and "Undo" is disabled |
