## Objective
Palette and size choices affect the next strokes on every canvas (US-006 AC1, AC2).

## Preconditions
A drawing turn with one guesser.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drawer picks red and size 40, draws a stroke | Drawer and guesser both show a thick red stroke |
| 2 | Drawer picks blue and size 4, draws a stroke | Both show a thin blue stroke; the red stroke is unchanged |
