## Objective
Nicknames are required and unique in the room (US-002 AC2, AC3).

## Preconditions
A room with host "Sam".

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the invite link and choose "Join" with an empty nickname | Join is refused with "Please enter a nickname" |
| 2 | Enter "Sam" and choose "Join" | Join is refused with "Sam is taken, choose another nickname" |
| 3 | Enter "sam " (different case, trailing space) | Also refused as taken |
| 4 | Enter a 21-character nickname | Input stops at 20 characters |
