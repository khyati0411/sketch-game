## Objective
Both end conditions work and the reveal shows the word and points (US-013 AC1–AC3).

## Preconditions
Test timers; a game with drawer and two guessers.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Both guessers guess correctly | The turn ends at once, without waiting for the timer |
| 2 | The reveal shows | Every player sees "The word was …" and each player's points for the turn |
| 3 | Next turn, nobody guesses | The turn ends when the timer reaches 0 and the reveal shows |
