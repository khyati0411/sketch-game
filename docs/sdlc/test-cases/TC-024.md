## Objective
Each player draws once per round and the game ends after round 3 (US-014 AC1–AC3).

## Preconditions
Test timers; 3 players who joined in order A, B, C.

## Steps
| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Let every turn time out; record who draws | The order is A, B, C, A, B, C, A, B, C |
| 2 | Watch the header | It shows "Round 1 of 3", then 2, then 3 |
| 3 | After the ninth turn | The results screen shows and no new turn starts |
